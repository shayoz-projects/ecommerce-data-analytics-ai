## E-commerce Data Analytics & AI Project
## Product Requirements Document- Public Version

### 1. Business background
The project is based on a small boutique Wix e-commerce business that sells newborn gift boxes and individual baby products   through a Wix-based storefront (business name withheld from this public repository). 

The business is independently managed by its owner, who is currently responsible for all operational activities, including product management, order processing, customer service, marketing, and business decision-making.

As the business continues to grow, reviewing increasing amounts of data and identifying meaningful trends manually becomes more time-consuming. The purpose of this project is to support the owner by transforming business data into clear insights, prioritized recommendations, and practical actions.

The public version does not disclose the business name, absolute revenue, customer information or real product names.

## 2. Business Problem

Wix provides basic information such as revenue, average order value, and top-selling products. However, the business does not currently have a structured process for:

- Understanding the reasons behind changes in revenue and order volume.
- Comparing product vs. box performance over time.
- Analysing repeat purchases and customer behaviours.
- Identifying products that are frequently purchased together.
- Turning calculated metrics into practical business actions.

## 3. Project Goal

The goal is to build an analytics and decision-support process that transforms Wix data into reliable business insights and proposed actions.

The project will combine:

- SQL for business analysis and KPI calculations
- Python for data cleaning, validation, and automation
- Power BI for dashboards and visual analysis
- An LLM for explaining validated findings and proposing possible actions
- Human review before presenting or implementing recommendations


## 4. Main Business Questions

### Sales

- Why did revenue or order volume change?
- Did changes come from the number of orders, average order value, or customer behaviour?
- Are the changes temporary or part of a longer trend?

### Products

- Which products and boxes contribute the most to business performance?
- How does the performance of gift boxes compare with individual products?
- Which products are frequently purchased together?
- Which products may require further investigation?

### Customers

- What percentage of customers make repeat purchases?
- Are there differences between new and returning customers?
- How long does it usually take for a customer to purchase again?
- Is a large share of revenue generated by a small group of customers?

### Business Actions

- Which products may be suitable for promotion?
- Which product combinations may support cross-selling?
- Which findings are strong enough to support a business experiment?
- Which questions require additional data?

## 5. Proposed Process

1. Export available data from Wix
2. Clean and validate the data
3. Store the data in a structured database
4. Calculate KPIs using SQL and Python
5. Analyse sales, product, and customer trends
6. Present the results in Power BI
7. Send only validated, aggregated metrics to the LLM
8. Generate explanations and proposed actions
9. Review the AI output for accuracy and unsupported claims
10. Present the final findings to the business owner

## 6. Initial KPIs

The first version may include:

- Total revenue
- Number of completed orders
- Average order value
- Revenue and order growth
- Average items per order
- Repeat-customer rate
- New versus returning customers
- Revenue and units sold by product
- Gift-box versus individual-product performance
- Frequently purchased product combinations
- Cancellation or refund rate

Additional metrics such as discounts, website traffic, and conversion will depend on the available Wix data.

## 7. AI Requirements

The AI layer must:

- Use only metrics provided by the analytical layer
- Receive aggregated data rather than raw customer data
- Separate facts from possible explanations
- Avoid presenting assumptions as confirmed conclusions
- Show the evidence supporting each insight
- State when the available data is insufficient
- Present recommendations as proposed tests, not guaranteed outcomes

## 8. Project Scope

### Possible Future Development

- Direct Wix API integration
- Automated data refresh
- Forecasting
- Website traffic and conversion analysis
- Automated alerts
- An interactive AI agent that lets the business owner ask questions about the data in natural language. 
- An interactive Streamlit interface, potentially as the front-end for the AI agent above.

## 9. Success Criteria 

The project will be considered successful if:

- The data is cleaned and validated correctly.
- The calculated KPIs can be verified against the source data.
- The analysis provides useful information beyond the basic Wix reports.
- AI-generated insights are based only on validated metrics.
- The business owner identifies at least one proposed action that is worth testing.
